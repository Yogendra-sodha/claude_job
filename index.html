Welcome to your job-search cockpit 👋

One place to find jobs, tailor every application, write outreach, and track it all — without re-typing your life story on every form.

Your 3-minute workflow

  1. Fill My Profile once (only ever once).
  2. Open Job Feed → jobs arrive pre-scored & ranked for you.
  3. Paste a job description into Generate.
  4. Get a fit score, tailored resume bullets, cover letter, referral + recruiter messages.
  5. Hit Save to Tracker. Done. Next job.

Why this beats "auto-apply bots"

Mass auto-apply bots get accounts banned and get filtered out by ATS + recruiters because they're generic. Interviews come from tailored applications. This tool keeps quality high while cutting the 45-min-per-job grind to about 3 minutes. Referrals also boost interview odds massively — so we make outreach one click too.
